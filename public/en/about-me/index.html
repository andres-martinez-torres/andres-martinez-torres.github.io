A bit about myself #

Originally from Andalusia (Spain), I am a Lausanne-based researcher specializing in the computational analysis of online radicalization and group polarization. Currently, I am pursuing my Ph.D. at the University of Lausanne and working within the interdisciplinary People-Opinion Networks project. My research integrates signaling theory with computational methods, specifically social network analysis, NLP, and LLMs, to analyze inaccessible and radical online groups.

My academic background includes a BSc with First Class Honours in International Politics from City St George’s University London (UK) and an MSc in Crisis and Security Management from Leiden University (The Hague, Netherlands), with a specialisation in the governance of crises. Prior to my Ph.D., I had the privilege to contribute to Human Rights Watch’s advocacy efforts at the UN Human Rights Council. My technical toolkit centers on Python and R, supplemented by SQL and Tableau. Learn more about my research here.

Outside of academia, I am an experienced choir singer and perform with multiple vocal ensembles in the Lac Léman region.
